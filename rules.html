1-1 Simple Present and Present Progressive

SIMPLE PRESENT: Expresses general statements of fact, general truths, and habitual or everyday activities.

  • Water consists of hydrogen and oxygen.
  • I get up at seven every morning.

PRESENT PROGRESSIVE: Expresses an activity that is in progress right now at the moment of speaking, or temporary activities.

  • The students are sitting at their desks right now.
  • I am taking five courses this semester.

1-3 Verbs Not Usually Used in the Progressive (Stative Verbs)

Some verbs describe states, not actions, and are rarely used in progressive tenses.

Common Stative Verbs: know, believe, understand, recognize, like, hate, prefer, belong, possess, contain, consist of.

Example: I know your cousin. (INCORRECT: I am knowing your cousin.)
